# A1 Level Concepts (CEFR Beginner)

This document defines the vocabulary categories and concepts that should be covered at the A1 level. Each language implements these concepts with culturally appropriate, native-sounding vocabulary.

## Target Size
- **50-100 words** total for A1 level
- Phrases should be ≤6 words for clear pronunciation

## Categories

### 1. Greetings & Farewells (~10 words)
- Hello / Hi
- Good morning
- Good afternoon/evening
- Good night
- Goodbye
- See you later
- Please
- Thank you
- You're welcome
- Excuse me / Sorry

### 2. Essential Responses (~5 words)
- Yes
- No
- Maybe
- I don't know
- I don't understand

### 3. Numbers 1-10 (~10 words)
- One through ten
- (A2 extends to 100)

### 4. Common Nouns (~15 words)
- Person / People
- Man / Woman
- House / Home
- Water
- Food
- Day
- Night
- Time
- Name
- Friend
- Family
- Work
- Money
- City / Town
- Country

### 5. Basic Verbs (~10 words)
- To be
- To have
- To go
- To come
- To want
- To need
- To eat
- To drink
- To speak
- To understand

### 6. Common Adjectives (~10 words)
- Good
- Bad
- Big / Large
- Small / Little
- New
- Old
- Beautiful
- Easy
- Difficult
- Important

### 7. Question Words (~5 words)
- What
- Where
- When
- Who
- Why
- How

## Notes for Language Implementers

1. **Articles**: For languages with articles (Italian, Spanish, French, German), include the appropriate article with nouns
2. **Gender**: Mark grammatical gender where applicable
3. **Phrases**: Choose natural, commonly-used phrases that a beginner would actually hear
4. **Cultural fit**: Prefer culturally appropriate greetings over literal translations
