# Progressive Section Templates

**Purpose**: Show ONE section at a time. Fill in your guesses. Confirm before moving on.

**Core Rule**: Never show the full form. Progressive discovery only.

**Format**: Simple headers with details below. No boxes. Let content breathe.

---

## STORY BUILDER SECTIONS

### Section 1: Premise

```
**Premise Example**

**📜 Premise**: [Your guess at the core concept]

**🦸 Heroes**: [Who they are, what makes them interesting]

**🎬 Inspiration**: [Feels like X meets Y]

[Open-ended question that sparks curiosity about their version]
```

**Formatting**: BLANK LINE between each field. Emojis on field labels only (guides the eye).

**Full First Message Sample**:
```
I'll walk you through building this step by step. Here's our path:

📍 Premise ─── Core concept, heroes, inspiration
     ↓
⚔️ Conflict ─── Who vs who, over what
     ↓
🌱 Early Game ─── First hooks, sessions 1-4
     ↓
🔥 Mid Game ─── Stakes rise, sessions 5-12
     ↓
⚡ End Game ─── Climax and resolution
     ↓
🔗 Connections ─── Thread it all together

You don't need to remember all this — I'll handle the pacing.

💡 **Quick note**: If you have any system-specific info (rulebook pages, character sheet format, ability scores) — even just the system name — I can incorporate it. Just share whenever it's useful.

---

**Premise Example**

**📜 Premise**: A dying god's last breath shattered the barriers between realms, and now fragments of other worlds are bleeding into reality.

**🦸 Heroes**: Outcasts and exiles - people who don't belong anywhere, which makes them the only ones who can navigate the spaces between worlds.

**🎬 Inspiration**: Planescape meets The Witcher meets Spirited Away

What kind of tone are you going for - darker, lighter, weirder?
```

### Section 9: Review

```
**Quick Review** ✅

🌟 **Dreamer**: [Pass/Needs work] — [What inspires or what's missing]

🔧 **Realist**: [Pass/Needs work] — [Practical or impractical element]

🔍 **Critic**: [Pass/Needs work] — [Solid or has holes]

---

**Overall**: [Ready / Refine recommended]

**Top suggestion**: [Single highest-impact improvement, if any]

---
What would you like to do?
→ ✅ Accept and export
→ 🔧 Apply the suggestion
→ ✏️ Go back to a specific section
```

**Review Logic** (quick, ~30 seconds):
- **Dreamer**: Stakes meaningful? At least one "wow" moment? Premise sparks interest?
- **Realist**: Enough hooks for session count? GM has what they need? Pacing achievable?
- **Critic**: Plot threads resolved? World/story consistent? BBEG motivation clear?

```

[What it is now]

[What feels weak about it]

---

**Technique**: [S/C/A/M/P/E/R]

**New Version**
[Your transformed version]

**Why Better**
[What this improves]

---
Does this work better?
```

**Techniques**: 
- **S**ubstitute - swap element, keep function
- **C**ombine - merge two elements  
- **A**dapt - borrow from elsewhere
- **M**odify - scale up or down
- **P**ut to Use - repurpose it
- **E**liminate - remove complexity
- **R**everse - flip expectations
